Decisions

No issue found means you checked this item. Change needed needs an explanation and severity. A blocker prevents use or teaches/marks the wrong meaning. A major issue materially confuses or misleads; a minor issue is an editorial improvement. Unchecked items remain pending. The app may still have issues outside this packet.

For scripts, check prerequisites, context, valid alternatives and the answer rationale. For audio, listen for words, endings, stress, variety, speaker identity and naturalness. Listen on headphones and a phone speaker. Mark an item only after checking it.

Recordings

Named characters keep their own variety. Both reviewers should also check understanding of the other variety. A file hash identifies the audited recording; native quality has not been approved.
