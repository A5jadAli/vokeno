# Design system

The agreed direction, with the research behind it, is the [Vokeno Design Direction](https://claude.ai/artifact/5WZsftP3tEXaxybebUrjBV) page. The research itself is in `reports/` and `research_notes/`. This file records the rules the code follows and what has been built.

## Rules

Everything below lives in `src/constants/theme.ts`. A lint rule rejects colour literals, and literal font sizes, line heights, corner radii and spacing, anywhere else in `src/`.

- **Ink acts, colour identifies, lime speaks.** Every main action is ink with cream text on cream screens, and cream with ink text on dark screens. A language's colour marks where you are; it never fills a button. Lime (`Palette.lime`) is for voice only, and only on ink: the call and level-check microphones, the live voice line and the call invite's phone icon. On cream it is 1.02:1, which is invisible.
- **Language colours: three shades of one hue** (`trackColors`):
  - `tint` (OKLCH lightness 0.955), for card backgrounds;
  - `accent`, the fill for badges, dots, bands and decorative icons, with `onAccent` on it (white on Spanish violet, ink on the others). A new language uses lightness 0.74, where ink reads at 7.5:1 or more on every hue;
  - `deep` (lightness 0.47, chroma up to 0.13), for progress bars, small labels and "you are here" outlines: 5.5:1 or more on cream for every hue.
- **Selection is ink.** A chosen answer, option or language has an ink border or fill, never a language colour or yellow, so it cannot be mistaken for a language or for "nearly right". Choices carry a radio mark; right and wrong carry ✓ or ✗ and a haptic, never colour alone. Result sheets keep their tint and mark, but their button is ink like every action; where two choices sit side by side (review's "Not yet" and "I knew it") the quieter one is the white `secondary` tone.
- **One control per job.** `Segmented` (voka-ui) for switching between views of the same thing (lesson speed, exam parts, writing tasks); `LanguageSwitch` chips for choosing a language inside a screen; the header's language picker for moving between languages; radio cards only where each option needs a description (first run).
- **Right, wrong, nearly right** (`Feedback`): one background, fill and text colour each. White on each fill and each text colour on its background read at 4.5:1 or more.
- **Opacity is named by role.** On light screens: `Palette.line`, `soft`, `pressed`, `border`, `handle`, `shadow`, `scrim`. On dark screens: `OnDark.text`, `textSecondary` (0.72), `textFaint` (0.6, so small text stays at 6.3:1 or more), `fill`, `fillStrong`, `line`, `border`. `withAlpha` is private to the theme.
- **Type:** `FontSize` caption 12 · label 14 · body 16 · heading 18 · title 24 · display 32, each with one `LineHeight` (18 · 20 · 24 · 24 · 30 · 38). Bricolage Grotesque is for screen titles only (Profile's name, beside the avatar, at title size). Card titles stay at title size or smaller. Large numbers, badges and all text in the language being learned are Plus Jakarta Sans. Text keeps scaling with the phone's font-size setting.
- **Shape and space:** `Radius` sm 8 · md 12 · lg 16 · xl 24 · pill; `space(n)` is n × 4.
- **Icon tiles:** the language tint for language content (dialogues, conversations), grey (`Palette.soft`) for tools (skills, vocabulary, settings), and the language fill only for badges and status (a done mark, review due, a unit's badge).
- **Pick-one marks:** every single-choice option, answer or goal shows the same radio mark (`radiobox-marked` / `radiobox-blank`) on the right; ✓ is kept for "correct". The empty mark is `Palette.muted` (5.7:1 on white), never a faint opacity role, because a control needs 3:1.
- **Progress bars:** nothing done is an empty track; any progress shows at least a round cap.
- **Text never shrinks to fit.** No `adjustsFontSizeToFit`: buttons wrap to two lines and titles wrap, so the phone's larger text setting is kept.
- **The voice line** (`components/voice-line.tsx`) is the app icon's own curve (four Béziers, level ends), cut into about 60 equal round-ended pieces placed only with transforms, so its edge is smooth and it needs no SVG renderer, which 1.11.0 does not have. When the next native build adds `react-native-svg`, redraw it as one animated path. On a call it is the only thing that moves: its height is the real level of whoever is speaking, the microphone's `media-source` level or the coach's `inbound-rtp` level from the call's own WebRTC stats, polled ten times a second and smoothed on the UI thread. It is flat in silence (room hiss below half the speech threshold stays flat), lime from the coach's first word to the end of the call; before and after it, the still mark in `Dark.lineRest` stands in its place. The status text ("Your turn…", "Coach is speaking…") stays right under it, because one line cannot say whose voice it is. If an open microphone has picked up nothing at all in the call and reports exact silence for 8 seconds, the call says "We can't hear your microphone" with what to check. Once it has heard anything, silence is a quiet room: some phones' noise suppression reports exact zero then (vivo V2061). A phone that reports no level never sees the warning. Updates to the line are skipped when the level changes by less than 0.03, so silence costs no frames. With reduce motion on, it stands still: raised while someone speaks (held through 0.6 s pauses) and flat otherwise. Listen and start controls carry `VoiceLineMark`, the same shape standing still (lime while a voice plays). Never a made-up waveform.
- **The answer chime** (`features/feedback/sounds.ts`): 180 ms, loaded before the first answer and played with the haptic through `answeredRight()`, at media volume like the lesson audio. It is silent while a lesson voice plays and when Settings → Answer sounds is off, and plays once (it pauses before rewinding; a loop shipped and was fixed on 24 October 2026). It does not switch the audio mode to follow silent or vibrate: on Android, expo-audio's `setAudioModeAsync` also forces the speakerphone, which would move later calls to the loudspeaker, and it delayed the chime. Following the ringer needs a native check in the next store build. Matching pairs, sign-in and self-graded review keep the haptic only.
- **Course levels** are sections in each language's own range (`levelRange`: German and Spanish A1 to B2, English B1 to C1; lessons below the range are a "Warm-up before B1" inside the first level, never labelled with their level). Built by `courseLevels` in `journey/course.ts` and used by both Course and Progress, so the tabs always agree: a heading on the canvas with the level in a tint pill (deep text), its name (A1 Everyday basics, A2 Getting by, B1 Independent, B2 Confident, C1 Advanced) and one count from `levelProgress` (units when the level has units, lessons otherwise; done includes covered, and covered is said; the bar counts the same thing). The level you are in opens around "you are here" (one group before, two after, plus the next checkpoint) with "Show all"; others stay closed. Progress says "Working towards A1" until a level is reached. Each open level carries its honest note. A level with nothing built says "Coming later" and claims no starter set. Checkpoints sit between units as milestones, never padlocks: an ink-outlined row with a flag, filled with the date once passed ("Passed 24 Oct · 10 of 11 right first time"), or "Not passed yet" with the score. They lock nothing: opening a unit past one not yet taken (and not before the learner's own start point) shows one sheet, once per checkpoint, with two equal white buttons ("Take Checkpoint 1", "Start anyway"). A passed checkpoint covers the lessons before it (marked "Covered"). A level shows "reached" only by the rule in the Spanish A1 plan's "Checkpoint pass mark".
- **The streak** shows lit and unlit by shape: a filled flame or an outline (Ionicons `flame` / `flame-outline`), never a crossed-out sign.
- **Motion respects reduce-motion.** All animation uses Reanimated, whose animations and transitions follow the phone's reduce-motion setting by default (`ReduceMotion.System`). Keep that default: never pass `ReduceMotion.Never`, and never animate with React Native's `Animated`. No decorative motion and no pretend signals. The call header names the call ("Call with Ana", "Live practice"), never claims to be live.
