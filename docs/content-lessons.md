# Lessons from earlier units

Rules the content harness has learned from defects found in earlier units (`docs/content-pipeline.md`). Every author stage reads this file. A rule is added only when a unit's record shows the defect was real and could happen again elsewhere, and each one names the unit it came from. Remove a rule that turns out to be wrong, and keep the list short: the harness stops adding at 40.

## Rules

- State grammar rules with their applicable conditions, and qualify patterns that have exceptions rather than presenting them as universal. (from `es-a1-u6`, 2026-11-19: Unqualified noun-gender and plural rules were corrected.)
- Introduce and practise independently reusable new words and frames separately before combining them in a taught phrase. (from `es-a1-u7`, 2026-11-20: Bundled request, quantity and noun were separated and practised before combination.)
- Verify exam-task descriptions against official candidate and examiner materials, distinguishing required stages from optional follow-ups. (from `de-a1-u1`, 2026-11-20: Exam guidance misidentified task materials and made required follow-ups optional.)
- Check pronunciation tips against the example word’s actual sounds, and ensure learner-language respellings represent those sounds without ambiguous letter values. (from `de-a1-u1`, 2026-11-20: Pronunciation tips misrepresented a short vowel and an unstressed ending.)
