# Which apps get the most formal design-award recognition (as of October 2026)

Scope note: these notes rely on primary Apple pages (developer.apple.com/design/awards, Apple Newsroom) and Google's blog, plus a Wikipedia list for Apple Design Award (ADA) years 2016–2022. Those are the best-sourced bodies. Coverage of Webby, Red Dot, iF, Awwwards, D&AD and Fast Company is thin; see the Gaps sections. The research budget was about 16 tool calls.

## Q1. Apple Design Awards 2016–2026: winners, finalists, Apple's stated reasons, repeat winners

### Takeaway

Apple's own design award is the deepest and best-documented signal. Since 2021 it has six categories, and since 2023 each category has one app winner and one game winner. The developers Apple keeps returning to are Procreate (won 2022 and 2024), Lux Optics (Halide won the 2022 ADA, and the studio also won iPhone App of the Year in 2019 and 2024), Gentler Stories (finalist 2023, winner 2024, finalist 2026), algoriddim/djay (won 2016 and 2024), Condor Digital/Tide Guide (finalist 2023, winner 2026), Not Boring (won 2022, finalist 2026), Sago Mini (finalist 2023, winner 2026) and Klemens Strasser (finalist 2023, winner 2025). Apple's stated reasons repeat a few themes: intuitive or "effortless" controls, the app getting out of the user's way, delightful character and warmth, deep use of platform features (Live Activities, Watch, Siri), and accessibility built in from the start.

### Inferences

- ADA wins usually go to small independent studios, not big consumer brands. Duolingo, Headspace, Taobao and NBA are the exceptions. Apple's stated reasons rarely mention features. They mention feel: "effortlessly intuitive", "keep their focus on the counter rather than the screen", "delightful characters", "warmth and routine", "polish to data presentation and custom animations".
- Language learning has been rewarded three times in four years: Duolingo won Delight and Fun in 2023, CapWords won Delight and Fun in 2025, and Vocabulary was a 2025 Visuals finalist. Delight and Fun is where Apple puts learning apps.
- The 2026 cycle leaned heavily on Liquid Glass (iOS 26 design-language) adoption and visionOS. Tide Guide's citation names Liquid Glass explicitly.

### Gaps

- I did not verify ADA 2016–2022 against Apple's archived pages, and the Wikipedia list has no stated reasons for those years. Pre-2016 wins I remember but could not source in this session, such as Procreate (2013?) and Duolingo or Things in earlier years, should be treated as UNCONFIRMED.
- Full finalist lists for 2016–2022 were not collected.

## Q2. Apple App Store Awards / App of the Year and Google Play "Best of" (2017–2025): which recur?

### Takeaway

Editorial "App of the Year" picks overlap with ADA mainly through the same small set of craft-focused studios. Lux Optics, maker of Halide, won iPhone App of the Year twice (Spectre in 2019 and Kino in 2024) as well as the 2022 ADA. Tiimo went from 2024 ADA finalist to 2025 iPhone App of the Year. Focus Friend (Hank Green) was picked by BOTH Apple (2025 Cultural Impact) and Google (2025 Best App), the clearest cross-store recurrence in 2025.

### Cited Findings

- **2025 App Store Awards** (Dec 4, 2025; 17 winners from 45 finalists, judged on "innovation, user experience, and design"):

### Inferences

- Apps recognized by both Apple and Google editors in the same year (2025): Focus Friend, DREDGE, Chants of Sennaar and Pokémon TCG Pocket. Calm, the 2017 iPhone App of the Year, came back in Google's 2025 XR category.
- Apple's App of the Year rationales are mostly about capability ("AI tools", "film-inspired filters"). Only Strava's ("sleek design") and Pokémon's ("iPhone-friendly interface") name design outright. App of the Year is therefore a weaker design-specific signal than ADA.
- A language-learning app won a Google Play category in 2025 (Pingo AI, Hidden Gem).

### Gaps

- I did not collect App of the Year for 2020–2023 (from memory, possibly Wakeout! in 2020, Toca Life World in 2021, BeReal in 2022 and AllTrails in 2023; all UNCONFIRMED), Google Play Best of 2018–2024, or Apple's App Store Awards 2026, which have not been announced yet (they normally come in early December).

- Material Design Award winners for 2015, 2017, 2020 and after 2021 were not found. The program may have stopped after 2021, but that is unconfirmed.

## Q4. Cross-award recurrence: which roughly 5–10 apps or studios have the strongest institutional design recognition

### Takeaway

Based on verified evidence only, the strongest recurring design recognition belongs to these studios and apps, mostly independent craft studios rather than big brands:

1. **Procreate**: ADA 2022 and 2024, plus 2018 iPhone App of the Year for Procreate Pocket.
2. **Lux Optics (Halide / Kino / Spectre)**: ADA 2022, plus iPhone App of the Year 2019 and 2024.
3. **Duolingo**: ADA 2023 winner and finalist, plus a 2025 Webby.
4. **Gentler Stories (Gentler Streak / The Outsiders)**: ADA finalist 2023, winner 2024, finalist 2026.
5. **Tide Guide**: ADA finalist 2023, winner 2026.
6. **Tiimo**: ADA finalist 2024, iPhone App of the Year 2025.
7. **Be My Eyes**: ADA 2021 and App Store Cultural Impact 2025.
8. **Calm**: iPhone App of the Year 2017 and Google Play Best of 2025 (XR).
9. **Kitchen Stories**: Material Design Award 2016 and ADA 2017.
10. **(Not Boring)**: ADA 2022, finalist 2026.

Things 3, Flighty, Headspace, Darkroom, Carrot, Crouton, Bear and Airbnb each have one verified major design award in this evidence set.

### Inferences

- What the repeat winners have in common: a single, clearly focused job; custom illustration, character or tactile detail; deep native-platform integration (widgets, Live Activities, Watch, Pencil); and accessibility designed in from the start. Apple's citations repeat this almost word for word.
- Big consumer platforms like Instagram, Spotify, Airbnb, Notion and Figma barely show up in formal app-design awards after 2016. Their design reputation comes from the industry and the press rather than from juried app awards, though this is unverified for Webby, Red Dot and Awwwards.
- For Vokeno, the relevant precedents are Duolingo (2023 ADA Delight and Fun: "delightful characters and fun gamified experience") and CapWords (2025 ADA Delight and Fun: "a delightful way to learn independently while immersing users in their surroundings").

### Gaps

- Things 3, Linear, Arc, Notion, Stripe, Monzo, Revolut, Airbnb and Spotify could rank higher if Webby, Red Dot, iF, Awwwards or D&AD records were checked. This was not done.
- The 2026 App Store Awards and Google Play Best of 2026 had not been announced as of October 23, 2026.
